Module 1: Ethics & Culture

Support, Not Solve

As a student employee, your goal is to foster independence. We guide students toward the answer, but we never do the work for them. This protects academic integrity and builds true comprehension.

FERPA Reminder

Never discuss a student's grades, accommodations, or struggles with other students. Keep lab conversations professional.

Module 2: Inclusive Communication

The NTID Learning Center relies on clear, accessible communication. Because our lab serves Deaf, Hard-of-Hearing, and hearing students, flexibility and visual clarity are your best tools.

  • The Intake: Greet immediately. Use visual cues (waving) if they don't hear you.
  • Sightlines: Always ensure your face is well-lit and not blocked by monitors when speaking or signing.

The NTID Learning Center (located in 50B-A121) is packed with resources for NTID students. As an NLC employee, knowing every service is essential so you can direct students quickly and accurately.

Module 6: Online Learning & Video Tools

Recording, Zoom & Technology

Students frequently ask for help with online tools. Understanding Zoom best practices, Panopto recording, and technology requirements lets you provide fast, accurate support.

Panopto Key Fact

Panopto can only be used when an instructor creates an Assignment folder in myCourses specifically set up to accept Panopto videos. Students cannot use Panopto independently without this setup.

Before Zoom Meetings

  • • Solid background; keep lights shining in front of you
  • • Dress appropriately — no pajamas
  • • Computer on a desk or table (not your lap)
  • • Video ON, microphone MUTED by default
  • • Head and upper body visible in the frame

During Zoom Meetings

  • • Raise your hand to share or ask questions
  • • "Pin" the interpreter by right-clicking their video
  • • Use "Side-by-side mode" to see shared content and video together
  • • Stay seated and attentive throughout the meeting
  • • Keep pets, kids, and roommates off camera

Technology Requirements

  • • Computer less than 5 years old, with at least 1 GB of RAM
  • • Reliable high-speed internet (broadband, cable, or fiber)
  • • Up-to-date browser: Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox
  • • Windows 365/10 or later, or Mac OS X
  • • Ability to record yourself for video assignments
  • • For tech issues, contact ITS Service Desk at (585) 475-4357

Module 7: Online Policies & Academic Integrity

RIT Policies Every Student Must Know

Students are bound by RIT's policies whenever they use RIT systems, attend classes, or submit coursework. As an NLC employee, understanding these policies helps you guide students and uphold the center's standards.

C08.2 — Code of Conduct for Computer Use

Do not share passwords. Ensure proper network security. Do not download or share copyrighted materials.

D08.0 — Student Academic Integrity Policy

Submit your own work. Do not cheat or plagiarize. Be familiar with RIT's Honor Code.

C6.0 — Policy Prohibiting Discrimination and Harassment

Demonstrate civility and respect to others. Be open to differing opinions. Use appropriate language in-person and online.

C3.0 — Intellectual Property Policy

Do not share course materials with others or upload them to unauthorized sites.

D15 — Educational Records (FERPA)

Students have the right to privacy regarding their educational records. Never share a student's academic information with others.
