Module 1: Ethics & Culture

Support, Not Solve

As a student employee, your goal is to foster independence. We guide students toward the answer, but we never do the work for them. This protects academic integrity and builds true comprehension.

FERPA Reminder

Never discuss a student's grades, accommodations, or struggles with other students. Keep lab conversations professional.

Module 2: Inclusive Communication

The NTID Learning Center relies on clear, accessible communication. Because our lab serves Deaf, Hard-of-Hearing, and hearing students, flexibility and visual clarity are your best tools.

  • The Intake: Greet immediately. Use visual cues (waving) if they don't hear you.
  • Sightlines: Always ensure your face is well-lit and not blocked by monitors when speaking or signing.

Module 3: Lab & Tech Mastery

Students will panic when technology fails right before a deadline. You need to be calm and efficient. 60% of lab issues are related to printing, logins, and basic hardware connections.

Shift Notes are Critical

If a scanner breaks, leave a note in the system so the next employee doesn't waste 20 minutes diagnosing the same issue.

Module 4: Situational Flow

Midterms and finals mean the lab will be full. Learning how to triage requests and de-escalate stressed students is what separates a good employee from a great one.

  • Triage: Acknowledge waiting students ("I'll be with you in 5 minutes") so they don't feel ignored.
